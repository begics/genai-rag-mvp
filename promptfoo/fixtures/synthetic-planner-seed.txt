Fictional observatory operations reference for RAG evaluation

This entire document is invented test material. It describes no real facility.

The North Dome observatory tests its Asterion telescope lens every 17 days. The technician records each completed lens test in the blue maintenance ledger.

The Asterion telescope uses a 12-centimeter calibration plate. The plate is stored in cabinet C-14 in the North Dome instrument room.

The visitor gallery opens at 09:30 and closes at 16:45 from Monday through Saturday. The gallery is closed to visitors on Sunday.

The observatory's backup projector is named Lumen-3. It is kept in storage room B-2 and its test image is a purple spiral.

When the North Dome weather sensor reports wind above 40 kilometers per hour, the outdoor viewing deck closes. The indoor visitor gallery remains open during that condition.
